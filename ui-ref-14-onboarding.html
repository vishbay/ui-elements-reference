Changelogs and progressive disclosure

Porting notes. Tours are the most over-used onboarding pattern — they are appropriate for a genuinely novel interface and almost nothing else. Prefer a checklist plus good empty states, which help on day thirty as well as day one. Always store which tours and changelog entries a user has seen, keyed per user rather than per browser, or people who switch machines get re-onboarded forever. And give every tour a Skip on step one, always visible.
