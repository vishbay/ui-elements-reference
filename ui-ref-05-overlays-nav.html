03 — Transient surfaces

Bottom sheets and toasts

Toasts enter from the edge they will exit toward and stack upward with automatic reflow. Sheets use a heavier spring than drawers because they travel further — matching easing across different distances makes the shorter one feel sluggish.

04 — Tabs

Underline, pill, boxed, closable, overflow and vertical

Six tab treatments from one markup shape. The indicator is a single absolutely-positioned element moved with a spring, which is what makes tabs feel native. Closable tabs promote a neighbour when the active one is removed; the overflow variant scrolls with fade masks and no scrollbar; vertical tabs are the settings-page form.

Porting notes. Every overlay here needs three behaviours to feel finished: close on outside click, close on Escape, and return focus to the trigger on close. The first two are wired above; the third matters for keyboard users and is a one-liner — store the trigger element on open and call .focus() on close. Command palettes should search actions and destinations together, not in separate modes.
