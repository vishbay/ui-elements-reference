Buttons & Controls

The full interactive vocabulary: button variants, sizes, groups, split and async states; text fields with floating labels and validation; switches, checkboxes, radios, segmented controls, sliders; and the advanced inputs — tag entry, autocomplete, dropzone and click-to-edit.

⚠ Reduce Motion is on in your OS. CSS animations and transitions are being suppressed.
01 — Buttons

Variants, sizes, groups and async states

Six variants cover almost everything: primary, secondary, ghost, subtle, danger, link. The async pair at the bottom matters most in ops tools — a button that transitions idle → loading → drawn checkmark, and destructive actions that arm in place instead of opening a modal.

Tags, autocomplete, dropzone, inline edit

05 — Filter bar & date range

Saved views, filter chips, add-filter popover, two-click calendar

The spine of every list screen. Saved views are tabs with counts; active filters are removable chips; the add-filter popover searches fields. The date-range control pairs presets with a calendar where the first click sets the start, the second sets the end, and clicking before the start swaps them. Chip and calendar logic together are under sixty lines.
