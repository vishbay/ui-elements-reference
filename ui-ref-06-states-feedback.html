01 — Empty & error states

The screens nobody designs until it is too late

Four distinct states that are usually collapsed into one generic message: nothing exists yet, nothing matches the filter, you lack permission, and something broke. Each needs a different action — clearing a filter is not the same as requesting access.

04 — Freshness & notifications

Data age and grouped alerts

Freshness decay is the pattern most auto-refreshing dashboards miss: the indicator must visibly degrade, or a stale screen looks identical to a live one. Relative timestamps update themselves on an interval instead of waiting for a re-render.

Bell with count, panel with tabs and day groups, inline actions, empty state

Porting notes. Give every empty state a distinct action — a shared “nothing here” screen teaches users nothing about what to do next. Freshness decay needs a real timestamp comparison, not a counter that resets on re-render, otherwise a stalled poller will keep reporting “just now” forever. Relative-time intervals should be cleared on unmount or they leak in a single-page app.
