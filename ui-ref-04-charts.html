Charts & Visualisation

Chart types beyond the basics: streaming time series, stacked areas, calendar heatmaps, Gantt schedules with a now-marker, waterfall reconciliation, Sankey flows, grouped bars and scatter plots — all hand-rolled SVG, no charting library.

⚠ Reduce Motion is on in your OS. CSS animations and transitions are being suppressed.
01 — Time series

Streaming lines and stacked areas

Both use the pathLength="1" normalisation so dash-offset drawing works regardless of viewBox or data range. For genuinely live data, append points and shift the transform rather than redrawing the path — redrawing restarts the draw-in animation every tick, which looks like a glitch.

Porting notes. Everything here is hand-rolled SVG to show the mechanics; in production use ECharts or Chart.js for anything with axes, tooltips and responsive resizing — you do not want to maintain axis tick logic. Keep the hand-rolled approach for the small, opinionated pieces: gantt rows, heatmaps, bullets and sparklines, where a charting library is heavier than the thing it draws. Never map data to a rainbow scale; one hue with varying alpha reads better and survives theme switches.
