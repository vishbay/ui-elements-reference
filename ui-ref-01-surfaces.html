Surfaces & Materials

Elevation, frosted glass, grain, ambient light, focus and lit edges, spotlights, textures, borders and scrims — every pattern rendered in light and dark simultaneously so the treatments can be compared directly. Zero dependencies. No color-mix(); vendor-prefixed for older Chrome.

⚠ Reduce Motion is on in your OS. CSS animations are suppressed — drifting blobs, sweeps and pulses will look static.

02 — Frosted glass

Backdrop blur, saturation & the inset highlight

Glass needs three things together: backdrop-filter: blur() saturate(), a translucent background, and an inset 0 1px 0 top highlight that fakes a lit edge. Drop the highlight and it reads as a blurry rectangle. Light glass uses white at ~55%; dark glass uses white at ~6%. The backdrop here is page content, because that is what glass sits over in a product — not a colour mesh.

03 — Noise & grain

Where grain is visible, and where it is pointless

An SVG feTurbulence overlay shipped as a data URI. Grain earns its place on tonal dark gradients — empty states, hero backgrounds, login screens — where 8-bit banding shows on any large display. Over a saturated colour it disappears; over a flat surface it is a texture choice, not a fix. Left half of the band is the plain gradient, right half has grain at 20% normal blend on dark and 50% multiply on light; production values sit lower, the demo is turned up so you can see it.

04 — Ambient light

Two blobs, one hue, behind a card

The realistic descendant of the gradient mesh: two blurred circles at 20% opacity, accent and slate, drifting over 18–22 seconds behind a login or empty-state card. The card is opaque; nothing is read over the light. This is as far as a modern product goes — never behind data, never more than two hues, never above 25%.

05 — Light & edges

Shadow, focus, pulse, lit edge, shimmer

The five ways a surface signals state without a coloured glow. Depth is a dark blurred shadow; focus is a ring, not a halo; live is a pulsing dot; a lit edge is the accent fading into the hairline; shimmer belongs to skeletons. None of these are animated at rest except the two that mean something is happening.

08 — Borders & hairlines

Alpha borders, not solid colours

Define borders as rgba over the surface rather than a fixed grey. They then work at any elevation and in both themes without a second token. Accent-edge cards are the cheapest way to encode severity in a list.
